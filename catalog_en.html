Matcha — Mai Matcha

Grades

Mai Matcha — 100% shade-grown tea leaves from the highlands of Northern Vietnam. Processed by a partner factory with 20 years of export experience to Japan, every batch is tested by an ISO/IEC 17025-accredited laboratory. Actual measured values for the Premium line: EGCG 6.05 g/100g and L-Theanine 889 mg/100g — competitive with leading Japanese ceremonial matcha. We advise the right grade for each application: beverage, bakery, ice cream.
