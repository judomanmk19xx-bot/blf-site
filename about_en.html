メッセージ・想い

BLF aspires to be a bridge that delivers the agricultural and marine products of Vietnam — nurtured by the rich natural bounty of Bac Lieu Province and by the tireless efforts of its people — to our customers in Japan.
We place "Safe, Reliable, High Quality" at the very top of our priorities. Built on strong relationships of trust with local producers, we are committed to sustainable sourcing and stable supply.
Going forward, we will continue to stay close to the needs of the Japanese market, create new value, and contribute to the development of the food cultures of both Vietnam and Japan.
