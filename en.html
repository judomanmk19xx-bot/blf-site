About Us

As a Vietnamese Food Processing & Export Company

A Bridge Delivering
Vietnam's Natural Bounty to Japanese Customers

Bac Lieu Fisheries JSC (BLF), headquartered in Bac Lieu Province, Vietnam, is a comprehensive food company specializing in frozen foods, tea products, agricultural raw materials supply, and OEM Manufacturing manufacturing. With abundant natural resources, state-of-the-art facilities, and internationally-certified quality management, we deliver safe, reliable, premium-quality products to tables worldwide.
