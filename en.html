About us

A Vietnamese food processing & export company

Bac Lieu Fisheries JSC (BLF) is a comprehensive food company based in Bac Lieu, Vietnam, processing and exporting frozen foods, tea products and agricultural & aquatic produce. For years we have supplied high-quality products to the Japanese market.

Our end-to-end quality management system — from sourcing to production and shipment — earns our customers' trust and peace of mind.

FAQ

Is there a minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
It varies by product — please contact us. Small orders are welcome.
Can you provide samples?
Yes, free and paid samples are available. Please let us know which product you need.
What is the OEM lead time?
Depending on specification and quantity, typically 4–8 weeks from order to delivery.
Do you have quality certifications?
We hold international HACCP, ISO 22000 and food-safety certificates. Contact us for details.
Which export documents do you provide?
We support the full document set: CO, phytosanitary certificate, health certificate and more.
Do you support Japanese labels & packaging?
Yes — OEM production with label design compliant with Japanese labeling regulations.
