FAQ

Answers to frequently asked questions about doing business with us.

Is there a minimum order quantity（MOQ）?
The MOQ varies by item and specification. Please let us know the items and quantities you have in mind — we are happy to discuss flexibly.
Can you provide samples?
Yes. We can prepare samples so you can verify quality. Please contact us with the item, quantity and delivery destination.
What is the lead time for OEM orders?
Lead times depend on the item, quantity and packaging specifications. Once we understand your requirements, we will propose a concrete schedule.
What are your payment terms?
Bank transfer（T/T）is our standard method. Detailed terms are discussed individually after we hear your requirements.
Which trade terms（Incoterms）do you support?
We support both sea and air freight, and can work under terms such as FOB and CIF. Please let us know your destination port and preferred terms.
What certifications do you hold?
We maintain a quality management system built on international standards, with HACCP, Food Safety and Quality Control certifications.
Is traceability available?
Yes. Our management system allows tracking from raw material sourcing through to product shipment.
Can you manufacture private-label（OEM）products?
Yes. We provide end-to-end support from development to manufacturing, tailored to your brand concept.
Can we visit your factory?
Yes, visits are welcome. Please contact us in advance with your preferred date and time.
How do we place an order?
Please contact us via the inquiry form. Our representative will guide you step by step — from proposal and quotation to sample preparation.
