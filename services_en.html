Services

From raw material sourcing to manufacturing, quality control and delivery — BLF supports your business with an integrated system and a flexible approach.

Raw Material Supply

Drawing on the rich natural bounty of growing regions such as Bac Lieu Province, BLF carefully selects and sources raw materials including vegetables, fruits, tea and seafood. Through strong relationships of trust with local producers, planned production and disciplined inventory management, we keep quality consistent and ensure stable supply. Full traceability from sourcing to shipment means our customers can use our materials with complete confidence.

OEM Manufacturing

BLF offers OEM manufacturing tailored to your brand and specifications. From recipe and specification proposals through production, quality control and packaging, we support the entire process — staying close to your needs and helping you build products that strengthen your brand value. Please contact us to discuss your project.

How to Do Business with Us

From first inquiry to delivery, we guide you through a smooth, transparent process.

  1. Inquiry — Contact us via the form or by email. Our representative will get back to you.
  2. Proposal & Quotation — We listen to your needs and prepare a proposal and quotation matching your items, specifications and quantities.
  3. Sample Review — We provide samples so you can verify quality first-hand.
  4. Contract & Production — After signing, we proceed with planned production under strict quality control.
  5. Delivery — We deliver reliably, on the agreed terms and schedule.
